# AI Dev Presentation — Talking Points

## Slide 1 — Title
- Frame this as a human‑led modernization effort with AI support.
- Emphasize speed and quality without sacrificing governance.

## Slide 2 — Agenda
- Set expectations: problem, learning journey, workflow shift, tech stack, demo, lessons, Q&A.

## Slide 3 — Summary
- Current tracking is fragmented across spreadsheets and reporting tools.
- Inconsistencies create rework and delays.
- We need a centralized app that keeps links between data sources intact.

## Slide 4 — Looking for a Solution
- We explored AI to accelerate development without disrupting existing teams.

## Slide 5 — Our First Attempt
- We tried a large, end‑to‑end AI build.
- Outcome: promising ideas, but not production‑ready.

## Slide 6 — It Didn’t Meet Needs
- The first output lacked the precision and alignment required for our workflows.
- This was a learning step, not a setback.

## Slide 7 — Why It Didn’t Meet Needs
- Prompts were too broad and lacked scaffolding.
- Architecture decisions were not yet defined.
- Output needed tighter review and iteration.

## Slide 8 — Changing the Workflow
- We built a thin, human‑designed foundation first.
- Then used AI to extend modules and fill in repeatable work.

## Slide 9 — AI Expands on What Is Already There
- Short, scoped requests produced reliable results.
- Each change was reviewed and tested.

## Slide 10 — Tech Stack
- Strong conventions make the stack AI‑friendly and maintainable.
- AI benefits from explicit models and predictable structure.

## Slide 11 — Demo
- Show the flow from data entry to reporting.
- Highlight auditability and role‑based collaboration.

## Slide 12 — Lessons Learned
- AI is most effective with human‑led architecture and clear patterns.
- Analysts and domain experts validate outcomes.
- AI struggles when requirements are vague or conflicting.

## Slide 13 — Where AI Helped Most
- Boilerplate and repeating patterns.
- Consistent APIs, serializers, and UI components.
- Documentation and refactoring for clarity.

## Slide 14 — Areas Requiring Human Ownership
- Architecture trade‑offs.
- Nuanced business logic.
- Running and validating command‑line workflows.

## Slide 15 — Q&A
- Invite feedback from app, data, and analyst teams.
- Reassure: AI assists, but ownership and governance stay with us.
